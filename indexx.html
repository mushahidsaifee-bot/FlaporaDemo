About Altis Fitness Club

MORE THAN A GYM. A PLACE TO BECOME STRONGER.

Built for focused training in Betal Nagar, Kondhwa Budruk, Altis Fitness Club combines modern strength and cardio equipment with a welcoming, disciplined workout floor where every session counts.

Whether you are picking up a barbell for the first time or dialing in your compound lifts, our clean space, well-maintained machines, and supportive trainers give you the structure and form guidance needed to progress safely.

  • Positive and motivating workout environment
  • Dedicated strength, powerlifting, and cardio equipment
  • Clean, well-maintained floor and spotless washrooms
  • Guidance on proper exercise techniques for beginners and experienced lifters
